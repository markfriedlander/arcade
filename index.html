Arrows

No wrong moves.

Tap an arrow with a clear run to the edge and it slides out. Clearing one can only ever free others, never trap them, so there is no wrong order and no way to get stuck. Just find the one that can leave.

House rules

No ads, at all

Not a banner, not an interstitial, not a video you have to sit through to keep playing. The games are the whole page.

Nothing leaves your device

No accounts, no analytics, no servers, no trackers. Scores and saved boards live in your own browser and go nowhere else.

Phone and laptop

Built for touch and for a mouse in the same file. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app.

Works with the signal off

Each game is a single self-contained page with no dependencies. Once it has loaded, it keeps working on a plane.

Free and open

MIT licensed. Read it, fork it, host your own copy. There is nothing hidden in there.

Made to be left

Nothing nags you to come back. No streaks to protect, no daily reward, no reason to open it except wanting to.

Games that end when you decide to stop.
